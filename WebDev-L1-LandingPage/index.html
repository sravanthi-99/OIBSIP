Popular Destinations

Kerala

Top destinations like Munnar for tea hills, Alleppey for houseboat backwaters, Wayand for green forests and kochi for a historic port city charm.

Chennai

Iconic landmarks like the 13km-long Marina Beach, the historic Kapaleeswarar Temple in Mylapore and the colonial-era Fort St.George.

Ooty

Offers scenic spots like the Government botanical Garden, Ooty Lake for boating, Doddabetta Peak for panoramic views and the cascading Pykara Waterfalls.

Mumbai

Iconic landmarks like the Gateway of India, scenic seaside stretches along Marine Drive, historic architecture at Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus and the sea-bound marble shrine of Haji Ali Dargah.
